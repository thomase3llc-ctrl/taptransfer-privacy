# Privacy Policy for TapTransfer

**Effective Date:** July 14, 2026

Thank you for using **TapTransfer**.

TapTransfer is designed to help users transfer files and selected data directly between nearby Android devices. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how the app uses your information.

## Information We Collect

TapTransfer does not require you to create an account or sign in.

The app does not operate its own cloud storage or servers for transferring your personal data.

Depending on the features you choose to use, TapTransfer may request access to:

* Photos and videos
* Files and documents
* Folders that you select
* Contacts (only when you choose to transfer contacts)
* SMS and MMS messages (only when you enable Advanced Message Migration and use those features)
* Nearby devices, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and related permissions needed to discover and communicate with nearby devices
* Internet access for Google AdMob advertisements

These permissions are requested only to provide the features you choose to use.

## How Your Information Is Used

TapTransfer uses your selected data only to perform the actions you request, including:

* Transferring files between nearby devices
* Transferring selected folders
* Exporting and importing contacts
* Exporting and importing SMS and MMS messages when enabled
* Creating local transfer reports
* Verifying transferred files for integrity
* Displaying advertisements through Google AdMob

Your selected files and transferred content are processed locally for these features and are not uploaded to TapTransfer-operated servers during normal use.

## Data Sharing

TapTransfer does not sell your personal information.

The app does not upload your files, contacts, messages, or other transferred content to servers operated by the developer.

When you initiate a transfer, data is sent directly between participating devices using the available local transfer technology.

## Advertising

TapTransfer includes Google AdMob to display advertisements in the free version of the app.

Google may collect certain information, such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, diagnostic information, and other data as described in Google's own Privacy Policy in order to provide and measure advertisements.

TapTransfer does not receive or store this advertising information on its own servers.

## Permissions

TapTransfer requests permissions only when they are needed for the features you choose to use.

Examples include:

* **Nearby Devices, Bluetooth, and Wi-Fi** – to discover and connect to nearby devices.
* **Photos, Videos, Files, and Folders** – so you can select content to transfer.
* **Contacts** – only when transferring contacts.
* **SMS and MMS** – only when you enable Advanced Message Migration and use message transfer features.

You can deny permissions; however, related features may not function correctly without them.

## Internet Connectivity

TapTransfer transfers your selected data directly between nearby Android devices and does not rely on TapTransfer-operated cloud servers for normal transfers.

An internet connection may be used for:

* Loading advertisements through Google AdMob
* Downloading updates from Google Play
* Accessing Google services required for normal Android app functionality

## Data Security

We take reasonable steps to help protect your information during local transfers.

While no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, TapTransfer is designed to minimize unnecessary exposure of your data by transferring content directly between devices rather than through developer-operated cloud servers.

## Children's Privacy

TapTransfer is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through developer-operated services.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date.
